Standards-compliant ULPINs

14-digit Bhu-Aadhaar assembled from state, district, sub-district, village and an auto-incrementing plot number — with LGD state codes resolved by reverse geocoding.

Accurate 3D floor modelling

How it works

Three steps from a place name to a full vertical property registry.

1
1

Locate

Search any place through Nominatim, or drop the crosshair yourself. Choose a scan radius and the console previews the exact area it will process.

2
2

Identify

Overpass returns every building footprint with its real tags. Shapely computes the true area and centroid, then a 14-digit ULPIN is allocated per parcel.

3
3

Extrude

The footprint is extruded storey by storey in Three.js, and every flat receives a 20-digit unit ULPIN you can export straight to GeoJSON or CSV.
